# Camera review lessons V1 — living-room human feedback

## Purpose-based views

Use five distinct jobs: HERO (complete layout), REVERSE (space connections), DEPTH (circulation and depth), ARCHITECTURAL (openings/built-ins plus room context), COMPLEMENTARY (a genuinely different informative view). These are intent categories, not mandatory furniture close-ups. Small rooms may use a useful vanity/work zone detail if it adds information; do not force a wide view that invents space.

## Before room delivery

- Write the unique decision each view supports and its expected visible anchors.
- Compare all five as a contact sheet. Replace duplicate content, not merely duplicate camera positions.
- HERO shows the main furniture group, its usable circulation and at least one room connection; avoid aggressive foreground crops.
- REVERSE and DEPTH must differ by visible connections and circulation, not just by a small yaw change.
- ARCHITECTURAL includes enough room/furniture to locate windows, doors and built-ins in context.
- COMPLEMENTARY shows what HERO leaves unclear. Reserve product close-ups for useful design details, not as filler.
- Camera must sit at a real usable floor/landing location, outside walls/fixtures. Maintain upright verticals using Lens Shift.
- Increasing focal length narrows the frame. Solve excessive cropping with a feasible camera move or direction change before changing focal length. Do not blindly apply numeric suggestions.
- Check both geometric camera positions/FOV on plan and actual rendered previews. A plausible coordinate is insufficient.
- Keep accepted view parameters intact while repairing only requested views. Retain earlier previews and revisions.
- Deliver one room at a time for human camera review before producing its expensive style variants.
- Freeze approved matrix, lens, sensor, shift, source revision and resolution across FAITHFUL/DESIGNER/CREATIVE. AI visual outputs still require drift review.
