# Iteration 01 review — ARCH

## Visual failures

The living seating reads as an L-shaped cluster: the 3-seat sofa forms the west edge, the 2-seat sofa caps the north edge, and the lounge chair sits near the shared center. The cluster is legible, but the lounge chair is too central and the two-seat sofa sits close to the north edge. The dining table and four chairs are coherent, yet the north pair lies on the direct line toward the retained kitchen.

## Spatial reasons

The dining table belongs west of the retained U-shaped kitchen and the living seating faces the east projection wall. The first placement preserves the entry and stair paths, but the dining north chairs narrow the kitchen approach. The living cluster leaves a large flexible center for children, exercise and future mobility, although the lounge chair makes that center less clear than it needs to be.

## Seating as a group

Yes, it reads as a group at a glance. The three-seat sofa and two-seat sofa make an L and the lounge chair completes a loose conversation triangle. The chair should move to the edge so the center stays open.

## Empty or fragmented

The family room is intentionally open for flexible use, but the furniture island is slightly fragmented toward the west/north edge. The dining group is compact and belongs to the kitchen extension. There are no furniture overlaps.

## Dominant hierarchy

The intended hierarchy is retained kitchen and dining as the service anchor, an edge-loaded family seating island facing the east projection wall, and a clear entry-to-stair-to-balcony circulation spine. The furniture gives the seating island visual priority while keeping the split-level stair visible.

## CAD changes required

1. Move the two north dining chairs north to clear the kitchen approach line; keep the four-seat everyday topology.
2. Move the two-seat sofa slightly south/east to make the living group more conversational without entering the stair or north-balcony paths.
3. Move the lounge chair toward the east edge of the seating island so the center remains a play and mobility field.
4. Keep the three-seat sofa facing the east projection wall.
5. Request a compact movable coffee/side-table block because the approved A0.4 set has no living-room table.

Measured evidence: eight furniture inserts, zero furniture overlaps, and all transformed bounding boxes pass.
