# C-type Home — S1 Design Brief v01

**Status:** DRAFT_HUMAN_REVIEW  
**Stage boundary:** S1 records facts and requirements only. Layout coordinates and formal CAD belong to S2 or later.

## Household and intent

- Permanent residents: owner (38) and mother (68).
- Periodic residents/visitors: wife, child, relatives and guests.
- The home is long-term self-use with aging-in-place and flexible family return.

## Program

- Mother’s primary bedroom with an aging-in-place primary bathroom.
- Owner’s secondary primary bedroom with a private three-piece bathroom.
- Formal study first, with backup sleep capability.
- Guest room for periodic child/relative use.
- Shared public/guest bathroom.
- Two-level laundry strategy: main wash/dry on the north balcony and small-item wash capability in the guest/public bath.

## Public zone

The living room is a flexible family room. Conversation, child activity, elder-friendly movement and north-balcony connection have priority. Projector/laser-TV capability is required, but television is not the spatial center. Dining uses four daily seats and stays linked to the retained kitchen without choking G-DIN-LIV. The nominal dining table is about 1500 × 600 mm.

S1 does not choose sofa count, table orientation, lounge-chair use or furniture coordinates. Those are S2 functional-design questions.

## Existing and fixed conditions

- Kitchen KEEP.
- Existing walls and openings remain immutable for this phase.
- Existing stair remains.
- Existing ramp remains a provisional assisted-mobility provision and requires field verification.
- G-DIN-LIV sliding glass partition remains.
- North balcony is currently **OPEN_NOT_ENCLOSED**. Any enclosure is future proposed work and cannot be treated as existing area.
- Existing fixed cabinets and registered storage remain source constraints.

## Mother’s accessibility priorities

The mother’s bedroom-to-bath route should have assisted-mobility potential. The primary bathroom should prioritize a low/zero-threshold shower, wide entry, grab bars, fold-down seat, hand shower, dry/wet separation and night safety. S1 records the need; S2 and field verification decide the spatial solution.

## S2 questions

S2 must answer:

1. What is the public-zone focal hierarchy when the room is not TV-centric?
2. How do seating, child activity and the entry–stair–balcony spine form one spatial group?
3. How does dining relate to the retained kitchen and G-DIN-LIV?
4. Which negative spaces have a specific use?
5. How should the mother’s bedroom and primary bath form an assisted route?
6. How can the study stay a real study while providing backup sleep?
7. What secondary-bath door and service routing are feasible?
