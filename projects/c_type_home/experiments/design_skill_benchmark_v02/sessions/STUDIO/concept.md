# STUDIO dominant concept

## Calm diagonal hinge

The public zone is organized by one diagonal hinge from the kitchen/dining edge toward the living-to-balcony opening. Dining stays close to the existing kitchen workflow, while the three-piece living group turns around the hinge and leaves a generous central activity field. The hinge is a spatial device rather than a decorative line: it aligns the dining table, the primary sofa, and the lounge chair so the family can shift between meals, conversation, play, and projection without a fixed TV wall.

- **Anchor:** everyday family flexibility and aging-in-place movement.
- **Image:** a quiet hinge joining kitchen service to the bright balcony edge.
- **Device:** edge-loaded furniture with a single diagonal sightline through the public zone.
- **Form:** one diagonal from the dining/kitchen transition toward the north-balcony opening; no second geometric gesture is required.

The layout uses only approved A0.4 blocks. It protects the balcony route and keeps the center furniture-light. The tradeoff is that the diagonal leaves a deliberate asymmetry: the short sofa and chair sit farther from the kitchen, which favors conversation and a clear route over a symmetric composition.
