# Targeted style comparison after Faithful acceptance

Historical proposal superseded on 2026-10-09: Designer keeps main room/furniture and adds integrated soft furnishing; Creative explores new furniture/layout inside the simple pale-natural-wood aesthetic. See living_style_pilot_v4 and v5 contracts. V3 material-only pilot rejected. Status: proposals only. Designer and Creative generation is disabled by GENERATION_POLICY.json. No result is claimed visually distinct until an owner-reviewed single-view comparison proves it.

The former variants used the same strong kitchen appearance anchor, pale wood palette, daylight and restrained styling. A few adjective and tabletop changes were easily absorbed into the common appearance. Shared structural constraints are necessary; shared appearance constraints must not dominate the intended design change.

Faithful retains the accepted room hero, furniture family, cabinet divisions and source layout. It adds believable materials, fabric weight and photographic rendering. Current successful Faithful images are kept; do not regenerate to impose a new style.

Future Designer/Creative should begin from the selected Faithful room image and matching geometry, with an explicit change ledger naming visible objects and surface regions. Kitchen appearance is no longer a common style reference for those edits. Keep camera, walls, openings, furniture count, locations, envelopes and clearances fixed. Avoid uniform whole-room recoloring.

Example future Designer edit: Change ONLY the visible tabletop to quiet warm-grey limestone, the named existing closed cabinet faces to warmwhite within their current seams, and existing cushions to tailored taupe linen. Preserve untouched wood surfaces, perspective and object dimensions. Show the material allocation clearly enough to identify it at thumbnail size.

Example future Creative edit: Change ONLY the selected existing upholstery regions to muted sage/ivory, the named existing closed panels to a fine weave surface within current seams, and existing tabletop surface to natural-edge ash within its footprint. Use directional daylight through the same window. Preserve every original opening, ceiling and furniture placement. Do not reuse the kitchen image as an appearance anchor.

These are proposed changes, not construction authority. The exact cabinet panel eligibility must be checked against the selected source view before generation. Other rooms need their own ledger; bathroom mirrors, toilets and plumbing never inherit living furniture changes. If differences remain subtle, revise one named region and compare again; do not launch three whole-house variants.
